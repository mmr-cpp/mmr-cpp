# MRI Introduction

## Primary Functionalities
- Provides User Interface.
  - entry of patient information,
  - patient handling, table controls, coil handling, landmarking, and patient communication
  - protocol management, organization, selection, and modification
  - management of the scan setup and sequencing
  - display of generated images and other information
- Setup and generate images– the system has a high level of automation in managing the scanning process.  
Functions includs:
  - self-calibration to adapt for patient to patient variation (setting scan conditions such as RF frequency, transmit power, shimming, receive gains)
  - image acquisition, reconstruction, storage and management
- Assist with maintenance– the system assists the FE and support personnel, by providing
  - performance measurement tools
  - internal self-diagnostics
  - configuration management
- Provide external interfaces to the outside world, including
  - information from HIS/RIS systems
  - image management (import/export, filming, archive, remote display)
  - remote support and service (system monitoring, remote diagnostics, etc.)
 
 ## Primary Users
- **The operator** is the primary clinical user.
- **The patient** is generally the scanning subject.   
- **The radiologist/site physicist** is the main consumer for the images produced by the images.
- **The field engineer (FE)** is the primary service interface for the system.  The FE helps to install the system, calibrates the system, maintains the system, and is the first line of system troubleshooting and repair. 
- **The support engineer(s)** are a secondary support level.  They guide the FE, assist with troubleshooting or remote monitoring, and get involved in diagnosis and repair for more complex system issues.

## Primary External Interfaces 
- **Siting** includes the MR suite and surrounds.
- **Electrical power and cooling** is supplied by the site, and must be maintained for proper system operation.
- **Connectivity** provides for information transfer to and from the external world.
